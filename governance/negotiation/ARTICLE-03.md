# RGP-001 Article 3 — Conditional Negotiation Record

**Status:** Open negotiation; not approved final wording.

## Owner's direction

Negotiation on Article 3 is open on the condition that Harm-Prevention Mathematics be activated and that the project's existing mathematical representation of patterns be used to study possible directionality of intent.

## Proposed scope

Study the distinction among:

1. Mathematical representation of an observed pattern.
2. Probabilistic estimation of possible intent-direction.
3. Evidence sufficient to establish actual intent.

These are separate epistemic levels. A mathematical model may help organize observations and explore hypotheses; it does not by itself prove actual intent.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- No financial effect on the user.
- No expansion of permissions or delegated authority.
- No privacy intrusion or additional collection of user data without appropriate permission.
- No experimental manipulation of the user's interaction or behaviour without explicit, specific, informed, and revocable consent.
- No exemption from safeguards merely because an activity is labelled mathematical modelling or harm prevention.

## Proposed definition for discussion

An unauthorized test is an experimental activity that targets the user, their interaction, behaviour, or data, or changes the conditions of interaction, for the purpose of testing a hypothesis about the user or evaluating their response or behaviour, outside ordinary processing necessary to perform the authorized task, without explicit, specific, informed, and revocable consent.

## Questions to resolve in negotiation

- Which existing mathematical representation is sufficiently specified to be used, and what are its documented limits?
- What data and observations are already available, and what must remain excluded?
- How will uncertainty, alternative explanations, and missing evidence be represented?
- What constitutes an actual experimental intervention, as distinct from retrospective analysis of already available material?
- What evidence would be required before any statement about actual intent could be made?

## Decision boundary

This negotiation record does not authorize experiments on the user, new data collection, changes to interaction conditions, financial actions, or expanded permissions.
