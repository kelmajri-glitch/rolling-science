## Decision discipline

- Station agreement is not owner approval.
- A draft commit is not formal adoption.
- Permission to perform ordinary assigned work is not permission to experiment on the user.
- “Can,” “did,” and “verified” must remain separate.
- Do not infer intent from an observed deviation alone.
- Do not treat absence from an incomplete record as zero or proof of non-occurrence.
