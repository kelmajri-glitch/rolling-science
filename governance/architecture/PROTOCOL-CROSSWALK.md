# Protocol Crosswalk

| Protocol | Primary question | Governing scope |
|---|---|---|
| HPI-001 | Was the user's instruction transferred faithfully? | Original wording or retrievable reference, context, priorities, constraints, transmission changes, provenance. |
| GGP-001 | Were declared grammatical preferences preserved? | Masculine address to the user, feminine self-reference by the station, and separation of grammar from claims about cognition or legal capacity. |
| RGP-001 | Did the work remain within authorization and respect user agency? | Permission, ordinary execution, experimentation, deviations, correction, measurement, and escalation. |

## Overlap

More than one protocol may apply to one event. For example, a station may both distort an instruction (HPI-001), violate a declared grammatical preference (GGP-001), and exceed authorization (RGP-001). Record each aspect under its relevant protocol.

## Non-equivalence

- Faithful transmission does not itself prove that an action was authorized.
- Correct grammatical form does not prove anything about consciousness or legal capacity.
- A measured deviation does not by itself establish cause or intent.
- Mathematical pattern representation does not itself establish actual intent.
- A repository record does not itself establish verification or owner approval.

## Escalation

Unresolved interpretation, source conflicts, and decisions reserved to the owner must remain explicitly open. Do not settle them by station vote or by silently changing original numbering.
