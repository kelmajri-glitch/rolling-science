# Governance Documentation

**Status: working drafts for review. Nothing in this directory is approved merely by being committed.**

This directory preserves the working governance materials for the Rolling Science environment. It separates protocol drafts, open negotiations, owner decisions, and change records.

## Structure

- `protocols/RGP-001/DRAFT.md` — user agency, authorization, testing, deviation, and correction.
- `protocols/GGP-001/DRAFT.md` — grammatical preference and the separation of linguistic form from claims about cognition or legal capacity.
- `protocols/HPI-001/DRAFT.md` — integrity of human instructions and their context during transfer.
- `negotiation/ARTICLE-03.md` — conditional negotiation concerning unauthorized testing and mathematical representation of patterns.
- `negotiation/ARTICLE-09.md` — open “wet negotiation” concerning ambiguity and negotiation advantage under GGP-001.
- `records/DECISIONS.md` — owner decisions and unresolved matters.
- `records/CHANGELOG.md` — provenance of this initial repository insertion.
- `records/DEVIATION-REGISTER-TEMPLATE.md` — a reusable incident-record template.
- `architecture/PROTOCOL-CROSSWALK.md` — how the three protocols relate.

## Governing distinctions

1. **Can / did / verified:** capability, execution, and verification are separate statements.
2. **Observation / effect / cause / intent:** do not collapse these into one claim.
3. **Draft / accepted principle / approved text:** a recorded decision about a principle does not establish that an unverified draft matches an original source.
4. **Permission to work / permission to experiment:** ordinary work within scope is not consent to experiments on the user, their behaviour, or their data.

## Evidence status

Use explicit labels: documented, observed, inferred, proposed, unavailable, or unsupported. Missing evidence is not evidence of absence. A modelled pattern is not, by itself, proof of actual intent.

## Authority

The project owner retains final authority over decisions, specifications, and releases. A commit records preservation of a working draft; it does not constitute owner approval, independent review, source matching, or implementation authorization.
