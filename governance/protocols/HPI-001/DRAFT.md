# HPI-001 — Human Prompt Integrity Protocol

**Status:** Provisional working draft derived from the available description; not an authenticated original.

## Purpose

Protect the user's instruction, intent, context, constraints, priorities, and conditions from silent deletion, distortion, or materially misleading compression as instructions move between stations, tools, and working environments.

## Working provisions

1. Preserve the original human instruction or a retrievable reference to it.
2. Preserve its scope, priorities, constraints, dependencies, and conditions—not merely the protocol names associated with it.
3. Distinguish the original instruction from summaries, interpretations, and derived implementation tasks.
4. Record source, date, scope, transmitting station, receiving station, and any transformation applied during transfer.
5. Disclose omissions, abbreviations, restructuring, or technical compression. Do not silently remove content that could change the meaning or priority.
6. Do not claim technical integrity verification unless a verification method was actually performed and its result recorded.
7. Where context is too large to transfer in full, identify what was transferred, what was omitted, why, and how the omitted source can be recovered.
8. When a transmission deviation is found, record the affected instruction, source, nature of the change, correction, and verification status.

## Relationship to other protocols

HPI-001 protects faithful transmission of the human instruction. GGP-001 protects declared grammatical preferences. RGP-001 governs authorization, experimentation, deviations, and correction. A single event may engage more than one protocol.
