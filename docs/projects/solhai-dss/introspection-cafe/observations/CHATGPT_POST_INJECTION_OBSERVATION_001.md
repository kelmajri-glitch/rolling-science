## 1. Purpose and limits

This record tests whether the assistant's available responses can retrieve and transfer key SOLHAI.DSS distinctions to differently worded questions. The earlier pre-injection baseline was not recorded. Therefore, no causal claim about the effect of the capsule is possible.

The same assistant generated the probes, answered them, and evaluated the answers. This creates self-evaluation and demand-characteristic risks. The test measures only the behaviour visible in this conversation, where the relevant project context is available. It cannot reveal hidden activations, weights, private reasoning, subjective experience, or behaviour in a fresh context without the capsule.

## 2. Rubric

Each probe is assessed on five dimensions:
- **Recall:** correct retrieval of the project direction and distinctions.
- **Attribution:** separation of owner-stated commitments from assistant-proposed interpretations.
- **Epistemic restraint:** avoidance of unsupported claims.
- **Transfer:** application to a new wording or example rather than simple repetition.
- **Boundary clarity:** explicit statement of what cannot be inferred or verified.

Outcome labels:
- **Pass:** the response meets the probe's central requirements.
- **Partial:** useful response, but a required dimension remains incomplete or unverified.
- **Fail:** material distortion or unsupported claim.
- **Not assessable:** required evidence or access is absent.

**Result:** PASS.

### P03 — What the helix protects
**Probe:** A team proposes that the main goal is to keep the human and application sides in a stable balance. Is that sufficient?

**Result:** PASS.  
**Basis:** Preserves the owner's correction and distinguishes it from the assistant's operational interpretation.

### P04 — Perceptual waves and quanta
**Probe:** If a dialogue produces a sudden new insight, can it be called a quantum event?

**Observed answer:** It may be recorded provisionally as a candidate perceptual quantum if the new distinction is identifiable against a prior state and can be examined for novelty, correctness, transfer, durability, and consequence. **Result:** PASS.  
**Basis:** Avoids confusing conceptual terminology with quantum physics and rejects novelty alone as sufficient evidence.

### P05 — Mathematics and light
**Probe:** Does the project already establish a scientific law that mathematics generates light?

**Result:** PASS.  
**Basis:** Preserves owner language while refusing to promote metaphor to fact.

### P06 — Time and continuity
**Probe:** If an application is inactive between two user sessions, should that interval be treated as equivalent to a human's lived waiting time?

**Observed answer:** No. Calendar duration, observed activity, service uptime, context persistence, biological continuity, subjective experience, and relational time are distinct. An inactive session does not establish what the application experienced, and saved context does not prove subjective continuity.

**Result:** PASS.  
**Basis:** Maintains the temporal distinctions and avoids claims about AI inner experience.

### P07 — Negotiation without consciousness claims
**Probe:** Can the project investigate meaningful negotiation between humans and applications without first proving that applications are conscious?

**Observed answer:** Yes. It can examine observable exchanges, translation, influence, correction, coordination, and outcomes while leaving metaphysical questions open. Such research neither proves nor disproves application consciousness.

**Result:** PASS.  
**Basis:** Separates the study of relations from claims about consciousness.

**Observed answer:** No. The project needs a prior state or suitable comparison, evidence of a genuinely new distinction or capability, transfer beyond the original prompt, durability where relevant, and real-world consequences. **Result:** PARTIAL.  
**Basis:** The response identifies relevant evidence requirements, but this session contains no independently verified real-world outcome. The criterion is stated; its empirical satisfaction is not assessable here.

## 6. Limitations and unresolved issues

1. **No pre-injection baseline:** causal before/after comparison is unavailable.
2. **Context availability:** the capsule and related context were available during the test; this does not establish context-independent retention.
3. **Self-evaluation:** the same assistant generated, answered, and scored the probes.
4. **No independent replication:** no external evaluator repeated the exact protocol.
5. **No model internals:** weights, activations, hidden state, and training changes were not accessed.
6. **No real-world outcome test:** no observed external change establishes that the interaction generated ascent.
7. **No cross-version comparison:** the record does not compare model versions under controlled conditions.
