# Mathematical Research: Gain, Loss, Damage, and دفعية

## Purpose

This collection is reserved for the gradual development and critical examination of mathematical approaches to benefits/gains and to losses/damage/ضرر. It is intended to complement, not erase, conventional output or gain analysis.

The phrase **رياضيات دفع الضرر** and the related working label **رياضيات دفعية** are preserved as research-program language. They are not presented here as an already formalized or validated mathematical theory.

## Foundational cautions

1. Do not assume that all outcomes can be represented by one scalar.
2. Do not treat damage as merely negative gain without testing the assumptions.
3. Preserve who receives a benefit and who bears a loss.
4. Preserve timing, duration, reversibility, and delayed effects.
5. Distinguish observed harm from projected risk and from counterfactual harm avoided.
6. State units, baselines, boundaries, and uncertainty.
7. Record what a model excludes and what information is lost by aggregation.

## Suggested research record

For each candidate model, document:

- Target question and decision context.
- Definitions and operational variables.
- Units, scales, and measurement method.
- Baseline and counterfactual.
- Time horizon and affected entities.
- Distribution of benefits and harms.
- Reversibility and thresholds, if empirically justified.
- Uncertainty, sensitivity, and missing data.
- Aggregation rule, weights, and information loss.
- Counterexamples and conditions under which the model fails.

## Current status

This is a methodological research area, not a completed formal system. No equations are adopted by this index. Candidate formulations must be separately defined, derived, tested, and criticized before being treated as results.
