# Research Archive

This collection holds research questions, literature records, evidence reviews, and reproducible research notes.

## Minimum record requirements

For each substantive research record, capture as available:

- Research question and scope.
- Claim or observation being examined.
- Source identity and stable locator.
- Author/organization, publication date, version, and access date.
- Method and population/context.
- Exact support offered by the source.
- Limitations, conflicts, and uncertainty.
- Classification: documented, inferred, proposed, unavailable, or unsupported.
- Links to any derivative analysis.

A citation is not proof by itself: inspect whether the cited source actually supports the specific claim.

## Separation of evidence levels

Keep primary source material distinct from secondary summaries and our own interpretation. Do not call an internal review "independent verification" unless it was performed independently and its method and sources are recorded.
